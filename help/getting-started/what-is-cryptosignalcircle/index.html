What is CryptoSignalCircle?

CryptoSignalCircle is a platform that helps you automatically track, analyze, and journal your crypto trades by securely syncing data from supported exchanges.

Instead of manually exporting CSV files or updating spreadsheets, CryptoSignalCircle connects to your exchange using read-only API access and keeps your trading data up to date.

Who is CryptoSignalCircle for?​

CryptoSignalCircle is designed for:

  • Traders who want a clear overview of their performance
  • Users trading on one or multiple exchanges
  • Anyone who wants to journal trades without manual work
  • Traders who care about security and data privacy

It works for both active traders and long-term investors.

What does CryptoSignalCircle do?​

Once your exchange is connected, CryptoSignalCircle automatically:

  • Syncs completed trades
  • Organizes them into a structured trading journal
  • Calculates performance metrics such as P&L and win rate
  • Helps you review past trades and decisions

All syncing happens automatically in the background.

How does it work?​

  1. Create a CryptoSignalCircle account
  2. Connect one or more exchanges using read-only API keys
  3. Trades are synced automatically
  4. Review your journal and performance in one place

You remain in full control at all times.

:::info Is CryptoSignalCircle safe? Yes. CryptoSignalCircle uses read-only API access.

  • No trading or withdrawal permissions
  • Encrypted storage of API credentials
  • Access can be revoked at any time from your exchange

Security & data protection :::

What CryptoSignalCircle is not​

To avoid confusion, CryptoSignalCircle:

  • ❌ does not execute trades
  • ❌ does not manage funds
  • ❌ does not provide financial advice

It is a trade tracking and analysis tool, not a trading bot.
