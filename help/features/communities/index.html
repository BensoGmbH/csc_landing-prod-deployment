Communities

What is Communities?​

The Communities feature is where members, traders, and moderators collaborate around a shared trading theme (for example: futures, altcoins, swing, or intraday). It gives each group its own space for member access, announcements, and signal distribution.

Why use it?​

  • Keep members organized in one structured place.
  • Control who can access premium or private community content.
  • Connect traders/signals to the right audience.

Core actions​

Create a Community​

Creating a community gives you ownership of a dedicated space with its own branding, rules, and access model.

  1. Open Communities.
  2. Select Create Community.
  3. Enter the community name, description, and image.
  4. Configure visibility and member settings.
  5. Click Create to publish it.

Join a Community​

How users join depends on the selected join mode:

Open Join​

Anyone can click Join and enter immediately.

Request to Join​

Users submit a request; owner/admin reviews and approves or declines.

Invite Only​

Only users which are invited can coin.

Management tips​

  • Owners/admins can change access mode later in Community Settings.
  • Define community rules early to reduce moderation overhead.
  • Use clear descriptions so users know who the community is for.

Impressions​

  • Have a quick overview with the most important information about a community
  • Overview of all shared signals in the community
  • Manage members and roles of your community
