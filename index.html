What is a crypto trading journal and why should I use one?

A crypto trading journal is a place where you keep track of your trades and your decisions. You write down why you entered a trade, how you managed it, and how it turned out. Over time, this helps you spot patterns, understand your mistakes, and become more consistent in your trading.

How is this journal different from using a spreadsheet?

Spreadsheets work, but they require a lot of manual effort and can get messy very quickly. This journal is built specifically for crypto traders. It automatically calculates your stats, shows your performance in clear charts, and lets you filter and tag trades so you can analyze them without extra work.

Can I import my trades automatically from exchanges or wallets?

Yes. You can connect supported exchanges and wallets via API and your trades will sync automatically. Once connected, new trades are added without you having to enter them manually.

Is my trading data a secure and private?

Yes. Your data is stored securely and encrypted. We do not sell or share your information, and only you can access your trading data. When connecting an exchange or wallet, users are required to use API keys with read-only permissions only. This ensures that no trades can be executed and no changes or other unwanted actions can be performed.
